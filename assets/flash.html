Flash: send your library with no network

Your iPhone shows a fast series of QR codes and this computer's camera reads them. Nothing goes over Wi-Fi or the internet.

This page needs the camera to read the codes on your phone's screen. The pictures stay on this computer.
Waiting for the camera
0 of 0 pieces 0 codes read
  1. The phone has to be paired with this computer once. If it is not, open your library, choose Pair a phone, and scan that code first. That works offline too.
  2. In Zone on the iPhone, open the computer sync settings and start Flash.
  3. Hold the phone so its code fills most of the square, screen brightness up. Keep it steady until this page says it is done.
